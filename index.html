    Who lives without detectable light?

    7,630,118 people — 59.2% of the inhabited population — live in cells that are inhabited but emit no detectable night-time light, against 7,301,192 in 2015. The largest concentrations are listed in the table; they are the natural starting point for a targeted off-grid or grid-extension programme. Depending on the noise floor (0.15–0.5 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹), this figure ranges from 7,757,162 to 7,877,624: that is the indicator's real uncertainty.

    How did the lit footprint change?

    The lit footprint moved from 8,226 to 14,718 km² (+78.9%), with 7,159 km² newly lit and 668 km² losing detectable light. The sprawl ratio of 0.89 indicates a pattern of densification rather than outward spread.

    Is light concentrating or spreading?

    Population-weighted inequality in light per capita across ADM2 units narrowed from 0.540 to 0.433 (Gini). 65% of total inequality sits between ADM1 regions and 35% within them. Initially dimmer units grew faster, indicating convergence.

    Which districts changed significantly?

    Median ADM2 growth in Sum of Lights is +16.1% per year. Growth is measured between the median of the first and last three years. 3 unit(s) exceed the robust +2.5σ threshold and 1 fall below −2.5σ; these are candidates for investigation, not confirmed findings — each should be checked against the gas-flare list, the product version and any known local event. 8 unit(s) with a very low initial base are shown but never flagged. Neighbouring districts grow alike (Moran's I = 0.25): flagged changes often cluster and should be read as regional rather than local phenomena.

    Any month-level anomaly?

    Monthly Sum of Lights shows a seasonal amplitude of 1.98× between the brightest and dimmest calendar month — a reminder that month-on-month comparisons are meaningless without seasonal adjustment. 19 observation(s) deviate by more than 2.5 robust sigma from their own month-of-year baseline.

    Limitations — read before citing

    • Thresholds: noise floor 0.25, urban core 10.0 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹; every 'unlit' figure depends on the first of these.
    • The brightest 1 % of pixels hold 59.8% of the national Sum of Lights.
    • Gas-flare candidates detected: 3. Verify against petroleum infrastructure before interpreting.
    • Night-time lights do not measure electricity access, GDP or population — every such reading is an inference.
    • Activity that emits no light is invisible here: subsistence agriculture, daytime informal trade, indoor activity.
    • No DMSP-OLS year is compared with a VIIRS year; the two are different physical quantities.
